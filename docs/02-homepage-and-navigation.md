# Homepage and Navigation

## Five-second test

A new visitor should understand:

- what the business sells
- who it is for
- why it is credible
- what to do next

## Homepage checks

- clear value proposition
- visible primary CTA
- useful category links
- delivery and payment cues
- trust signals
- readable mobile hero
- limited competing messages
- current and maintained appearance
- no important text embedded only in images

## Navigation checks

- clear category labels
- visible search
- usable filters
- breadcrumb or back-path support
- no dead-end pages
- helpful empty states
- consistent mobile menu
- sensible product sorting
- category pages that explain the range

## Common conversion leaks

- vague hero copy
- large decorative banner pushing products down
- hidden search on mobile
- too many menu choices
- business terminology customers do not use
- categories with no clear distinction
- no route back from an empty result
