# Trust and Checkout

## Trust checks

- visible business identity
- working contact information
- secure site connection
- clear payment methods
- delivery policy
- returns / exchange policy
- warranty information
- real reviews or credible proof
- maintained social presence where relevant
- consistent branding and language
- no broken pages or outdated notices

## Cart checks

- item, quantity, and price are clear
- item can be removed or edited
- shipping or extra charges appear early
- coupon field does not distract unnecessarily
- checkout CTA is obvious
- cart persists when appropriate
- stock changes are explained

## Checkout checks

- guest checkout where suitable
- only necessary fields
- correct mobile keyboard types
- Sri Lankan phone validation
- useful field labels
- specific inline errors
- address fields appropriate to local delivery
- payment options explained
- no surprise charges
- clear place-order button
- duplicate submission protection
- confirmation page with next steps

## Error message standard

Weak:

> Invalid input.

Better:

> Enter a valid Sri Lankan mobile number, such as 07X XXX XXXX.

## Payment trust

Explain:

- whether the customer leaves the website
- supported card/payment types
- whether an advance is required
- what happens after payment
- what to do if payment fails
- how long verification may take
