# WhatsApp and Payment Recovery

## WhatsApp's role

WhatsApp can support the buying journey, but it should not compensate for missing basic product information.

Good uses:

- answer product-specific questions
- confirm custom orders
- clarify delivery
- recover failed payments
- follow up on quotations
- support high-consideration purchases

## Inquiry workflow

Track:

- source page
- product
- inquiry time
- customer question
- assigned staff member
- follow-up status
- next action
- outcome

## Honest measurement

A WhatsApp button click may indicate interest.

It does not automatically prove:

- a message was sent
- a conversation happened
- the customer was contacted
- an order was completed

## Failed-payment recovery

Prioritise cases using:

- status eligibility
- age
- order value where available
- previous follow-up
- payment method
- customer contact availability

## Follow-up message structure

1. identify the order or product
2. acknowledge the issue
3. provide a simple next step
4. offer help
5. avoid pressure or misleading urgency

Example:

> Hi, we noticed the payment for your order was not completed. Your items may still be available. Reply here and we’ll help you complete the order or choose another payment method.

## Recovery measurement

Track explicit stages:

- eligible
- follow-up started
- customer reached
- retry requested
- recovered
- no response
- not recoverable
