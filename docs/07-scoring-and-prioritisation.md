# Scoring and Prioritisation

## Category scoring

Each category has a maximum weight.

Example:

| Category | Weight | Score earned |
|---|---:|---:|
| Homepage | 10 | 7 |
| Navigation | 10 | 6 |
| Product Page | 20 | 11 |
| Trust | 15 | 9 |
| Cart & Checkout | 20 | 12 |
| Mobile | 10 | 5 |
| Performance | 5 | 3 |
| WhatsApp | 5 | 2 |
| Recovery | 5 | 1 |
| **Total** | **100** | **56** |

## Finding priority

Rate each from 1 to 5:

- Impact
- Effort
- Confidence
- Urgency

```text
Priority Score = (Impact × Confidence × Urgency) ÷ Effort
```

## Action buckets

### Quick Wins

High impact, low effort.

### Strategic Fixes

High impact, medium or high effort.

### Evidence Needed

Potentially important, but more data is required.

### Nice to Have

Low impact or cosmetic.

## Recommended implementation order

1. Transaction blockers
2. Trust and payment uncertainty
3. Mobile CTA and form friction
4. Product-information gaps
5. Navigation and discovery
6. Follow-up and recovery
7. Visual polish
