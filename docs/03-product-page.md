# Product Page

## Product-page objective

Answer enough questions for the customer to take the next action confidently.

## Image quality

- clear main image
- consistent background
- multiple useful angles
- zoom or sufficient detail
- dimensions where relevant
- colour or material examples
- no misleading image differences
- mobile-friendly gallery

## Information hierarchy

Recommended order:

1. Product name
2. Price
3. Stock / availability
4. Key benefit
5. Primary CTA
6. Delivery summary
7. Main features
8. Specifications
9. Dimensions
10. Customisation / pre-order terms
11. Returns / warranty
12. Reviews or proof

## CTA checks

- visually distinct
- close to price and availability
- clear action wording
- large enough tap target
- visible on mobile
- not competing with several equal-priority buttons
- disabled state explained
- out-of-stock alternative provided

## Common objections to answer

- How long will delivery take?
- Is cash on delivery available?
- Is advance payment required?
- Can the colour or size be changed?
- Is installation included?
- What happens if the item is damaged?
- Can I return or exchange it?
- Is the photo accurate?
- Who can I contact before ordering?

## Custom and pre-order products

Make clear:

- expected lead time
- advance amount
- customisation limits
- final-confirmation process
- cancellation rules
- colour/material variation warning
