# Sri Lankan E-Commerce Context

## Delivery clarity

Customers may need clear information about:

- island-wide delivery
- delivery zones
- estimated days
- same-day or next-day availability
- bulky-item restrictions
- installation
- courier contact
- cash-on-delivery eligibility

## Payment clarity

Common concerns include:

- cash on delivery
- bank transfer
- card payments
- instalment or buy-now-pay-later options
- advance payment for custom or pre-order items
- manual payment verification
- payment-failure support

## Phone and address forms

Forms should support:

- Sri Lankan mobile-number formats
- practical address entry
- district or city information where operationally useful
- delivery instructions
- optional landmark field when justified

Do not add fields merely because they are common on generic templates.

## Language

Use language customers understand.

Depending on the audience, a clear Sinhala-English blend may work better than formal corporate English.

## Trust

Useful local trust signals may include:

- visible Sri Lankan contact number
- physical location or service area
- delivery terms
- recognised payment methods
- warranty or after-sales support
- authentic customer proof
- active support channel

## WhatsApp

WhatsApp is widely useful for support and high-consideration purchases, but the website should still answer the main buying questions before the customer is forced to message.
