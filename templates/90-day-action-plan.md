# 90-Day Conversion Action Plan

## Days 1–14 — Fix Transaction Blockers

- checkout errors
- payment failures
- broken mobile actions
- missing delivery or pricing information
- severe performance issues

## Days 15–30 — Improve Product Decision Support

- product images
- benefits and specifications
- stock and delivery clarity
- customisation and pre-order rules
- trust content

## Days 31–60 — Improve Discovery and Follow-Up

- navigation
- search and filters
- WhatsApp workflow
- abandoned inquiry follow-up
- failed-payment recovery

## Days 61–90 — Measure and Optimise

- event tracking
- baseline comparison
- usability checks
- A/B tests where appropriate
- repeat audit
- prioritise next cycle
